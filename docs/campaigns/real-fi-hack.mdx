# Privacy in Practice: Code the Future of Digital Freedom

Online privacy is often taken for granted or dismissed as abstract and unnecessary. However, in a world where surveillance and government overreach threaten citizens from Nepal to the UK, privacy provides a shield for non-violent resistance and civic action for positive change. 

From 23 September to 16 October, 2025, join the RealFi Hackathon to build solutions to problems in need of solutions, win bounties, and support those fighting for change. Your code can make privacy practical, approachable, and impactful.

Sign up now. A network of builders beats a nation of bureaucrats.

## How it works

RealFi Hack is a virtual hackathon for builders to create tools for real-world impact. Logos is supporting with bounties for the Privacy Infrastructure and Resilient Activist Technology challenges.

* **Sign Up:** Register at [RealFi Hack](https://realfi-hack.devspot.app/en?activeTab=overview) by 16 October, 2025.
* **Team Up:** Create a project, pick a challenge, and invite other builders to join your team.
* **Pick a Challenge:** Build with Waku, Nimbus, or Tor-compatible tech. Hunt Logos bounties.

  * **Resilient Activist Technology:** Create tools to shield activists from censorship and surveillance. Up to $4,000 for 1–2 winning projects.
  * **Logos x Tor Privacy Infrastructure:** Strengthen Tor's privacy capabilities with innovative infrastructure. Up to $4,000 for 1–2 winning projects.
* **Connect:** Join our community server to chat with our contributors. Follow [@Logos\_network](https://twitter.com/Logos_network) for updates.
* **Deliver:** Submit your prototype by the deadline to win bounties and drive real-world impact for activists globally.
* **Utilise:** Logos and activists worldwide need the tools we propose, which will be integrated and used by our community.
