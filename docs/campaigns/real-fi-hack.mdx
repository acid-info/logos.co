# Privacy in Practice: Code the Future of Digital Freedom

In a world where surveillance and censorship threaten citizens from Nepal to the UK, privacy shields non-violent resistance and civic action for positive change. 

From 23 September to 16 October, 2025, join the RealFi Hackathon by Logos and Funding the Commons to build tech with actual use cases, win bounties, and support those fighting for change. Your code can make privacy practical, approachable, and impactful.

Sign up now: [https://realfi-hack.devspot.app/](https://realfi-hack.devspot.app/)

## How it works

* **Sign Up:** Register now at [RealFi Hack](https://realfi-hack.devspot.app/en?activeTab=overview)
* **Team Up:** Create a project, pick a challenge, and invite other builders to join your team.
* **Pick a Challenge:** Build with Waku, Nimbus, or Tor-compatible tech. Logos has bounties for:

  * **Resilient Activist Technology:** For tools to shield activists from censorship and surveillance. Up to $4,000 for 1–2 winning projects.
  * **Logos x Tor Privacy Infrastructure:** Innovate to strengthen Tor’s privacy. Up to $4,000 for 1–2 winning projects.
* **Connect:** Join our [encrypted community server](https://status.app/c/G6gAAMT_mNvE4zy-g2YaEkeilMmfS-iUjBscsDf-Mo404Dgunu2Dbiy0NxEPswqXa39WE791XPf6j1VF_lYp_9HXE3WUxhWC1aeuH7yBDvDg2wBCYbFRiJCre6BgAGfMnWFd9m8YxlhRcKxCphN18b6hLh1W-CvG5ibjTl12I28f99fl_dnxjGgB#zQ3shhxxHeXxc1jb72h6LK6jSz4HfNLELYBKyhaDfZRji8cMa) to chat with our contributors. Follow [@Logos\_network](https://twitter.com/Logos_network) for updates.
* **Deliver:** Submit your project by the deadline to win bounties and drive real-world impact for activists globally.
* **Utilise:** Logos and activists worldwide need the tools we propose, which will be integrated and used by our community.
